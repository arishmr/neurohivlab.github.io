### culturally-grounded neuropsychiatric screening
We are interested in the development of mental health screening tools that are **culturally grounded and empirically validated**. Our work in this area has so far focused on developing (with co-production approaches) an [isiXhosa version of the PHQ-9](https://edin.ac/isixhosa-phq9) to enable screening for depressive symptoms in community, clinic, and research settings in South Africa.

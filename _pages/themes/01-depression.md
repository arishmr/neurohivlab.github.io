### mechanisms of depression in people with HIV
Our main area of interest is determining **what drives the higher rates of depression in people with HIV**. We focus on physiological processes - namely, inflammation and metabolic dysfunction - as potential contributors to this comorbidity. Our approach also recognises, however, that socioeconomic factors such as stigma, discrimination, and deprivation may play an important role in this relationship. This research may result in dual benefit: to enable more effective mental healthcare for this underserved community, and to improve our mechanistic understanding of depression in the general population.

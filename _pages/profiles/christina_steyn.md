**Christina Steyn**, PhD Candidate, Translational Neuroscience

Christina is a Wellcome Trust–funded PhD student in Translational Neuroscience. Her research explores whether people with mental illness have altered patterns of metabolism, including differences in the rhythmicity of metabolic processes due to circadian or menstrual cycle-related changes. Her work includes analysing data from large health datasets and conducting prospective clinical studies.

**Dr Arish Mudra Rakshasa-Loots**, Principal Investigator

I am a [KJCF Quest Fellow in Brain Health & Multimorbidity](https://www.kjcf.org.uk/fellowships-programme/) and Chair of the British Neuroscience Association's [EDI Advisory Board](https://www.bna.org.uk/about-us/our-governance/bna-advisory-boards/edi-advisory-board.html).

I was born and raised in Ghaziabad, India, which is where I completed my secondary education. I then completed a BA in Biochemistry, Neuroscience, and Ancient & Classical Studies from Earlham College (USA, 2019), a PhD in Translational Neuroscience from the University of Edinburgh (UK, 2024), MLitt in Creative Writing from the University of Glasgow (UK, 2026), and postdoctoral research in the UKRI Hub for Metabolic Psychiatry at Edinburgh (UK, 2024-26). I also previously held a Thomas J. Watson Fellowship (2019-20), through which I worked with HIV healthcare organisations around the globe. I have lived and worked in numerous countries on six continents, and this global experience shapes my approach to science, writing, and advocacy.
